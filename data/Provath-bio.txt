Md. Al-Mamun Provath is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). His research lies at the intersection of large language models, multimodal learning, and computer vision, with emphasis on robustness, security, fairness, and interpretability in AI systems. He is particularly interested in vision-language integration, cross-modal reasoning, and human-centered AI design, aiming to apply these methods in biomedical imaging, smart surveillance, and conversational AI.

He earned his B.Sc. in CSE from CUET (1st in class, CGPA 3.96/4.00) and is pursuing his M.Sc. in CSE at CUET (CGPA 3.87/4.00). His research has appeared in IEEE Access and leading international conferences, covering topics such as deep learning for violence detection, multimodal sarcasm and humor detection, image watermarking, cancer image classification, and explainable AI for security applications.

Provath has received the University Gold Medal, UGC Merit Scholarship, and Dean’s Award for his academic achievements. He is proficient in Python, Java, C, C++, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, and Django, with expertise in deep learning, computer vision, multimodal learning, and LLM reasoning. Alongside teaching, he has mentored undergraduate theses and delivered AI training under government and international initiatives such as the EDGE Project (World Bank & GoB) and the BDSET Project (BHTPA, ICT Division).

He also serves as the Moderator of the CUET Computer Club and has contributed to professional activities, including organizing the International Conference on Electrical, Computer, and Communication Engineering (ECCE).
